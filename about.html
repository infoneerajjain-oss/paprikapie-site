Our story

Two kitchens under one roof

PaprikaPie opened on M.G. Road in August 2023 with a simple idea: Safidon shouldn't have to choose between a proper masala dosa and a proper cheese-pull pizza. So we run both — a tawa on one side, a pizza oven on the other, and a menu that stretches from a ₹49 vada pav to a fully loaded Paprika Special Paneer.

Everything is pure vegetarian, and nothing is made in advance. Your dough gets rolled, your dosa hits the tawa and your chaap goes on the grill only once we've confirmed your order. It takes a few minutes longer. It tastes like it should.
